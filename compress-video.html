Why Compress Videos In-Browser?

Video files are often hundreds of megabytes in size, making them cumbersome to email, share via Slack, or upload to websites. Most online compressors force you to upload large, confidential video files to third-party servers, where they wait in processing queues and risk privacy leaks. OnlyFiles eliminates the server altogether, compressing the video on your device using hardware-accelerated browser engines.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does compressing video reduce audio quality?
OnlyFiles prioritizes speech clarity and audio fidelity while reducing redundant video stream bitrate, so dialogues and sound effects remain clear.
Can I compress video on my mobile phone?
Yes! Modern mobile browsers on iOS (Safari) and Android (Chrome) feature built-in hardware acceleration that supports client-side video processing.
