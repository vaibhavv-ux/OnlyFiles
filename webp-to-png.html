Why Convert WebP to PNG?

WebP was developed by Google to provide superior lossy and lossless compression for web images. However, many desktop programs, older photo editors, Discord bots, word processors, and print services still reject WebP files or fail to render transparent backgrounds properly. Converting your WebP files to PNG solves compatibility instantly without sacrificing visual quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does converting WebP to PNG reduce image quality?
No. PNG is a lossless format. When OnlyFiles converts your WebP image, it decodes the raw pixel data and encodes it into a standard PNG bitstream without any additional loss of fidelity.
Why is the PNG file size larger than the WebP file?
WebP uses advanced predictive encoding algorithms designed specifically to minimize web payload sizes. PNG is a lossless format designed for universal compatibility and editing flexibility, which is naturally larger in file size.
Are my private photos uploaded to OnlyFiles?
Never. OnlyFiles does not operate cloud storage or server-side conversion queues. All computation runs purely inside your browser on your device's CPU and memory.
