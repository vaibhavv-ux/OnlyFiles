Why On-Device Processing Matters

Popular online converters (like iLovePDF, Smallpdf, and TinyPNG) require sending your personal documents across public networks to remote cloud infrastructure. OnlyFiles executes 100% of processing in browser memory, working even when disconnected from the internet.
