Why Capture Video Frames with OnlyFiles?

Taking a screenshot of a paused video on your computer often produces blurry results cluttered with video player interface buttons, pause icons, and lowered display resolution. Standard screenshot hotkeys cannot capture the raw uncompressed video buffer.

OnlyFiles reads the underlying video decoding canvas directly. When you click capture, your browser generates a pristine PNG photograph rendered at the full native dimensions of the video stream (such as 1920x1080 or 3840x2160) without any UI artifacts.

Native Sensor Resolution

Captures the true pixel dimensions of the video frame, completely free of browser playback controls or pause icons.

High-Quality PNG Output

Saves extracted snapshots as lossless PNG files, preventing secondary compression artifacts or color banding.

Automated Sequence Mode

Set a custom interval to sample still frames across an entire sequence for motion studies, thumbnails, or storyboards.

Confidential & Local

Zero network round-trips. Your private footage is processed locally inside your web browser's memory.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I capture 4K video frames?
Yes. If your source video is 3840x2160 (4K UHD), OnlyFiles extracts still frames at the full 4K resolution regardless of your monitor's display scaling.
Is there any limit to how many frames I can capture?
No. You can capture as many individual snapshots as you like and download each directly to your computer.
