Why Compress Images In Your Browser?

Every traditional image compression site requires sending your personal photos, screenshots, or design assets to a remote cloud server. This exposes your photos to data breaches, unauthorized AI training scrapers, and long upload wait times.

OnlyFiles operates 100% on your device: Using the HTML5 Canvas API and modern browser codecs, your computer or phone re-encodes the image locally in RAM. No upload delay, no network bandwidth consumption, and zero privacy risks.

Private & Confidential

Family photos, sensitive screenshots, identity documents, and personal designs never leave your browser.

Instant Results

No waiting in cloud upload queues. Your local processor compresses high-res 20MP photos in under a second.

Next-Gen WebP Conversion

Convert hefty PNGs or JPEGs directly into modern WebP format for 30%–50% extra size reduction with identical visual clarity.

Automatic Metadata Scrubbing

Removes hidden camera models, timestamps, and GPS coordinates automatically during the compression process.
