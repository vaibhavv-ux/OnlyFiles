Why Convert JPGs to PDF On-Device?

Combining scanned contracts, medical invoices, receipts, and identification cards into PDF documents is a standard daily task. However, almost every free online converter forces you to upload your personal files to unknown third-party cloud servers where they are saved and vulnerable to data interception.

OnlyFiles completely eliminates this risk: using client-side JavaScript and the official pdf-lib WebAssembly binary, your images are scaled, encapsulated, and merged into a PDF document directly inside your browser memory. Your files never touch a remote server.

100% Client-Side Privacy

Your photos, receipts, and confidential scans stay on your device. Zero cloud uploads, zero data logging.

Instant Assembly

No waiting in cloud upload queues or processing delays. Assembly happens immediately using your computer's CPU.

Custom Page Sequencing

Easily arrange your photos in any order using simple up and down controls before generating the PDF.

Original Resolution Preserved

How to Convert JPG Images to PDF in 3 Steps

1. Select your images: Click the dropzone or drag and drop one or multiple JPG, PNG, or WebP files from your phone or computer.

2. Arrange page order: Use the arrow buttons next to each file to arrange your photos in the exact sequence you want them to appear in the PDF.

3. Generate and save: Click "Combine & Download PDF". Your new multi-page PDF is rendered in memory and saved to your device immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert multiple photos into one multi-page PDF?
Yes. You can select and upload multiple images at once. Each image becomes an individual page in your generated PDF document in the sequence you specify.
Are my private documents and scans secure?
Completely. Since OnlyFiles executes the entire PDF generation directly inside your web browser, not a single byte of your photos is ever sent over the internet or saved to our servers.
What happens to high-resolution camera images?
High-resolution camera photos are embedded with their full pixel fidelity intact. The page dimensions automatically match the aspect ratio of each photo to prevent distortion or clipping.
Does this work on iPhone and Android?
Yes. OnlyFiles works smoothly on all modern mobile and desktop browsers including Safari, Chrome, Firefox, and Edge without installing any external application.
