Frequently Asked Questions

Before emailing, here are quick answers to common questions:

  • Did my file get stored on your server? No. OnlyFiles is 100% client-side. Your files never touch our servers and are processed entirely in your web browser.
  • Can OnlyFiles recover a file I closed? No. Because we never receive or save your files, once you close the browser tab, any temporary in-memory data is permanently wiped.
  • Is OnlyFiles really 100% free? Yes. All 19 tools are completely free to use with no subscription or hidden charges.
  • Response Time: We aim to reply to all legitimate support emails within 24 to 48 hours on business days.
