Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete page 2 and page 5 from my PDF?
Set the action mode to 'Delete these pages', enter '2, 5' in the page numbers field, and click 'Apply & Download PDF'. The output will contain all pages except 2 and 5.
Does rotating pages reduce PDF quality?
No. The tool modifies only the rotation metadata flag of the PDF page objects. Text remains vector-sharp and embedded graphics are not recompressed.
