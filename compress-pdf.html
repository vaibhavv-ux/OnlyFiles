Why Compress PDFs Directly In Your Browser?

Traditional PDF compressors (like Smallpdf, iLovePDF, or CloudConvert) require you to upload your sensitive document to their remote web servers. Once on their infrastructure, your file is decrypted, processed, and held in cloud storage until deleted.

OnlyFiles works entirely differently: We use WebAssembly and modern browser memory to parse, downsample, and re-encode PDF streams locally. Your file never crosses the network wire — giving you the speed of local desktop software with the convenience of a web application.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does OnlyFiles store a copy of my PDF?
No. OnlyFiles has no backend storage bucket or document processing server. The compression code runs in your browser's V8 JavaScript engine. As soon as you close the tab, all memory is freed.
Will compression affect text clarity or links?
No. OnlyFiles optimizes embedded raster images and strips unreferenced metadata streams. All vector text, fonts, hyperlinks, bookmarks, and form fields remain intact and sharp.

Yes! PDF compression on OnlyFiles is 100% free with no watermark, no ads, and no account required. Multi-PDF merges, page extraction, and all 19 tools are 100% free with unlimited usage.
