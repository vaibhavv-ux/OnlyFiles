Why Convert JPG to PNG?

JPEG is an efficient, lossy compression format best suited for general photography where microscopic details can be discarded. However, editing a JPEG repeatedly leads to generational degradation and visible compression artifacts. Converting your JPG to PNG creates an uncompressed, lossless container ideal for editing, archiving, graphic design, and clean web displays.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does converting JPG to PNG make the image transparent?
No. Original JPG files do not contain transparency channels. The resulting PNG will preserve the existing opaque background, but allows you to edit and add transparency in image editing software without further compression artifacts.
Is my data secure?
Completely. OnlyFiles is designed from the ground up on a zero-upload philosophy. Your files are processed entirely in browser memory and discarded as soon as you close the tab.
