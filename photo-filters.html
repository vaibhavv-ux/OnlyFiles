How to Apply Photo Filters

1. Import Image: Click the dropzone or drag and drop any JPG, PNG, or WebP photo into the tool window.

2. Select a Style: Browse the live thumbnail preview strip below your image. Click any style to instantly preview it on your full photo.

3. Adjust Sliders: Fine-tune lighting and color balance using the Brightness, Contrast, and Saturation sliders.

4. Save & Export: Click "Download Filtered Image" to immediately save your finalized picture to your computer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does applying filters reduce the resolution or quality of my photo?

No. The filters are rendered at the exact native dimensions of your original image using HTML5 Canvas graphics acceleration, maintaining crisp details.

Are my personal pictures stored or transmitted across the internet?

Never. All adjustments, filters, and rendering operations execute purely inside your local browser sandbox. No photo is ever sent to an external server.

Can I adjust individual color settings like contrast and saturation?

Yes. You can select any artistic preset and further tweak Brightness, Contrast, and Saturation sliders in real time.
