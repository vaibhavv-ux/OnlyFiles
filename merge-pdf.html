Why Combine PDFs Locally?

Document merging often involves sensitive personal paperwork: merging tax receipts, combining identity documents with rental applications, or assembling corporate contracts. Uploading these documents to public cloud conversion servers exposes your private data to retention vulnerabilities.

OnlyFiles performs document concatenation entirely client-side: Using modern browser stream assembly, pages are copied and re-indexed directly in your device's memory. No file upload is ever initiated.
