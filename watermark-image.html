Why Watermark Photos Directly In Your Browser?

Sharing creative work, architectural blueprints, real estate listings, and photography online exposes your images to unauthorized reuse, scraping, and content theft. Watermarking establishes ownership and deters copyright infringement.

Most commercial watermarking tools require uploading your high-resolution files to remote cloud servers. OnlyFiles processes every single pixel locally in your browser tab using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your source photographs remain on your machine at all times.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does watermarking reduce the sharpness of my original photo?
No. The canvas draws the watermark directly onto the full-resolution pixel buffer of your image, preserving crisp focus and fine details.
Can the watermark be easily cropped out?
If you select the 'Tiled Diagonal' placement mode, the text repeats across the entire surface of the photograph, making it virtually impossible for someone to crop the watermark away without ruining the picture.
Are there any file size or daily usage caps?
None. Because all processing happens locally using your own hardware, you can watermark as many pictures of any resolution as you desire.
