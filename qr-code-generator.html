Why Static, Client-Side QR Codes Are Safer

Many online QR generators act as dynamic redirect brokers: they encode their own domain into the QR code, redirecting visitors to your target website. Months later, these services frequently lock your QR code behind an extortionate paywall, breaking restaurant menus, packaging labels, and marketing campaigns.

OnlyFiles creates pure static QR codes. Your destination URL is burned directly into the 2D pixel matrix. Because the generator executes 100% inside your browser, there is no middleman, no analytics tracking, and no expiration date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can any smartphone scan these QR codes?
Yes. Every iOS camera app, Android camera, and generic barcode scanner can scan standard static QR codes without special apps.
What is the difference between Error Correction levels?
Higher error correction (Level Q and H) allows the QR code to remain readable even if up to 25% or 30% of the graphic is damaged, smudged, or covered by a center brand logo.
